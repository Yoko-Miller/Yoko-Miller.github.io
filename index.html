Leon: The Professional

Narrative Prototype | Greybox | Level Design

Visual Vocabulary & Pacing

Transitioning the player from a claustrophobic, shadow-heavy corridor into an open rooftop arena. Color-coded blocks act as a non-verbal language: Red signifies immediate threat zones, while Green dictates safe pathing.

Level Topology & Signposting

A macro-view of the architectural structure. This layout allows for testing the distribution of sightlines and cover objects, ensuring the player is guided implicitly through the environment.

Atmosphere & Lighting

Exploring the Neo-Noir aesthetic of the 90s. Using sharp shadows and tight geometry to induce tension before the combat encounter.

Under the Hood: UE5 Blueprints

Technical implementation is key to delivering the narrative feel. I built custom logic for aiming transitions, fluid camera control, and movement inputs.
